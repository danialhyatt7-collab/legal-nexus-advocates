Questions

Before you get in touch

How much does a consultation cost?

We confirm the consultation fee when you book, before you come in. For many matters, the first short call to understand your issue is free.

Can I consult online if I'm overseas?

Yes. Overseas Pakistanis can consult over WhatsApp or video call, and we can handle matters in Islamabad and Rawalpindi through a power of attorney.

How quickly can you act in an urgent bail matter?

Call or WhatsApp us directly rather than using the form. Urgent criminal matters are taken up the same day wherever possible.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Your CNIC, plus any documents related to your matter: notices, FIR copy, agreements, property papers or court orders. If you're unsure, bring everything and we'll sort it.

Is what I tell you kept confidential?

Yes. Communications with your advocate are privileged and kept strictly confidential.
